# Employment agencies and businesses (curated quotes)

_GOV.UK mainstream guide for people who run an employment agency or
employment business, linked from the Fair Work Agency's pages. Curated
quotes cover the prohibitions, websites aimed at work-finding and the
limited-company opt-out; the full guide is at the source URI. Curated
2026-10-08; review by kylewelsby pending._

## Overview

> If you run an employment agency or employment business you have to
> follow certain rules.

> You cannot:

> charge a fee to a work-seeker for work finding services

> stop someone from working elsewhere or terminating their contract with
> you

> make someone tell them the name of any future employer

## Publications advertising work-finding services

> If you run a publication or website totally or mostly aimed at
> providing a work-finding service you’ll normally have to follow the
> rules for employment agencies and businesses.

> However, if job advertisements are only a small part of what is
> published then the rules probably do not apply.

## Limited company contractors and opting out of the rules

> Work-seekers registered as limited companies are also covered by the
> rules unless they say they want to opt out.

> Limited company workers cannot opt out if they’re going to be working
> with vulnerable people.
