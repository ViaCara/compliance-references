# CAP Code: Scope of the Code (curated quotes)

_The non-broadcast Code's own statement of what it covers and what it does
not. Curated quotes only; the full text is at the source URI. Reviewed
2026-09-11 by kylewelsby._

## I The Code applies to

> h. Advertisements and other marketing communications by or from
> companies, organisations or sole traders on their own websites, or in
> other non-paid-for space online under their control, that are directly
> connected with the supply or transfer of goods, services, opportunities
> and gifts, or which consist of direct solicitations of donations as part
> of their own fund-raising activities.

## II The Code does not apply to

> h. private correspondence, including correspondence between
> organisations and their customers about existing relationships or past
> purchases

> k. editorial content; for example, of the media or of books and regular
> competitions such as crosswords

> q. website content not covered by I d and I h, including (but not
> limited to) editorial content, news or public relations material,
> corporate reports and natural listings on a search engine or a price
> comparison site
