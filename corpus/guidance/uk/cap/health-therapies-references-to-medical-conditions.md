# Health: Therapies and references to Medical Conditions (CAP AdviceOnline, curated quotes)

_CAP AdviceOnline advice given by the CAP Executive about non-broadcast
advertising. It does not constitute legal advice and does not bind CAP, CAP
advisory panels or the ASA. Last updated 01 Sep 2022. Curated quotes only;
the full advice is at the source URI. Reviewed 2026-09-04 by kylewelsby._

> Any claim that a particular therapy can diagnose, treat, help with or
> cure a medical condition is unlikely to be acceptable unless the
> advertiser is considered suitably qualified to diagnose or treat that
> symptom or condition (Rule 12.2) and holds robust evidence to support the
> efficacy claim (Rule 12.1).

The advice gives worked examples of unacceptable claims and acceptable
alternatives, including:

> "Many of my clients suffer from depression, especially in winter, and
> find that Shiatsu can help lift their mood"

revised to:

> "Many of my clients find Shiatsu excellent for improving their mood"
