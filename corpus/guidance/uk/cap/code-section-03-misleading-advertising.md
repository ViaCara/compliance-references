# CAP Code Section 3: Misleading advertising (curated quotes)

_The UK Code of Non-broadcast Advertising and Direct & Promotional Marketing
(the CAP Code), administered by the Committee of Advertising Practice (CAP)
and enforced by the Advertising Standards Authority (ASA). Curated quotes
only; the full section is at the source URI. Reviewed 2026-09-04 by
kylewelsby._

## General

Rule 3.1:

> Marketing communications must not materially mislead or be likely to do
> so.

Rule 3.3:

> Marketing communications must not omit material information or
> information required to be included by law.

## Substantiation

Rule 3.7:

> Before distributing or submitting a marketing communication for
> publication, marketers must hold documentary evidence to prove claims
> that consumers are likely to regard as objective and that are capable of
> objective substantiation.

## Qualification

Rule 3.9:

> Marketing communications must not mislead by omitting significant
> limitations and qualifications.

## Exaggeration

Rule 3.11:

> Marketing communications must not mislead consumers by exaggerating the
> capability or performance of a product.
