# CAP Code Section 12: Medicines, medical devices, health-related products and beauty products (curated quotes)

_The UK Code of Non-broadcast Advertising and Direct & Promotional Marketing
(the CAP Code), administered by the Committee of Advertising Practice (CAP)
and enforced by the Advertising Standards Authority (ASA). Curated quotes
only; the full section is at the source URI. Reviewed 2026-09-04 by
kylewelsby. Rule 12.6 completed to the full rule from the live page on
2026-11-18; review of that change pending._

## Definition

> "Disease" includes any injury, ailment or adverse condition, whether of
> body or mind.

## Rules

Rule 12.1:

> Objective claims must be backed by evidence, if relevant consisting of
> trials conducted on people.

Rule 12.2:

> Marketers must not discourage essential treatment for conditions for
> which medical supervision should be sought.

Rule 12.6:

> Marketers should not falsely claim that a product is able to prevent or
> treat disease or a malformation; restore, correct or modify a
> physiological function; or modify a person’s appearance.* (This rule
> reflects a prohibited practice from Schedule 20 of the DMCCA. See note in
> Section 3 for more details).

Rule 12.12:

> Prescription-only medicines or prescription-only medical treatments may not be advertised to the public.

Rule 12.20, opening sentence:

> Homeopathic medicinal products must be registered in the UK.

Rule 12.20 concerns homeopathic products. It does not supply the general
prescription-only advertising restriction. Rule 12.12 supplies that rule.
Consult the full rule at the source URI for its remaining conditions.
