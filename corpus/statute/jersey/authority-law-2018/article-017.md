# Jersey Data Protection Authority Law 2018 Article 17 - Registration (curated quotes)

_Registration sits in a companion statute to the substantive Data Protection
(Jersey) Law 2018: the Data Protection Authority (Jersey) Law 2018. Quoted
here from the JOIC regulator's own registration guidance restating the
Article 17 duty, cross-referenced against Article 6(1)(b) of the Data
Protection (Jersey) Law 2018 ("if established in Jersey, may process
personal data ... only if the controller is registered under Article 17 of
the Authority Law"). A clean machine-readable copy of the Authority Law's
own consolidated PDF was not located during this pass; treat the section
number as regulator-confirmed, not yet cross-checked against the bare
statute text. Reviewed 2026-09-18 by kylewelsby._

## Registration duty (JOIC guidance, restating Article 17)

> A controller or processor established in Jersey must not cause or permit
> personal data to be processed without being registered as a controller or
> processor under Article 17.

## Application

Gated on establishment "in Jersey" (see also Art.6(1)(b) of the Data
Protection (Jersey) Law 2018). A controller with no Jersey establishment, in
scope only through Article 4(2)(c) (`article-004.md`), does not trigger this
duty on that basis alone.
