# Jersey Data Protection Authority Law 2018 Article 17 - Registration (curated quotes)

_Data Protection Authority (Jersey) Law 2018, Official Consolidated Version
(1 January 2019 - Current). Official text at the source URI. Curated quotes
only; paragraphs (3) to (5) on the application, the fee and the register are

## Article 17(1), (2) and (6)

> 17 Registration of controllers and processors
> (1) A controller or processor established in Jersey must not cause or
> permit personal data to be processed without being registered as a
> controller or processor under this Article.
> (2) However, Regulations may make such exemptions from the requirements to
> register under this Article as the States think fit.
> (6) A person who contravenes paragraph (1) is guilty of an offence.

## Application

The registration duty is gated on establishment "in Jersey" (Article
17(1); see also Article 6(1)(b) of the Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018).
A controller with no Jersey establishment, in scope only through Article
4(2)(c) of that Law (`statute/jersey/dpjl-2018/article-004.md`), does not
trigger this duty on that basis alone.
