# Jersey DPJL 2018 Schedule 2 Part 2 para 6 - Consent (curated quotes)

_Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018, Schedule 2 ("Conditions for
Processing"), Part 2 ("Conditions for Processing Personal Data and Special
Category Data"), engaged via Article 9(2) for special category data.
Official text at the source URI. Reviewed 2026-09-18 by kylewelsby._

## Schedule 2, Part 2, paragraph 6

> 6 Consent
> The data subject has given explicit consent to the processing for one or
> more specific purposes.

## Application

Article 9(2) requires at least one Part 2 condition for special category
processing. Paragraph 6 is the basis ViaCara's triage consent flow relies on
for Jersey-resident mental-health data.
