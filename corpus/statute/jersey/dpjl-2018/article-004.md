# Jersey DPJL 2018 Article 4 - Application (curated quotes)

_Data Protection (Jersey) Law 2018, Official Consolidated Version (25 Nov
2020 - 5 May 2023). Official text at the source URI. Curated quotes only,
paragraphs (1) and (4)-(7) omitted. Reviewed 2026-09-18 by kylewelsby._

## Article 4(2)-(3)

> (2) This Law applies to the processing of personal data -
> (a) in the context of a controller or processor established in Jersey;
> (b) by a controller or processor not established in Jersey but who uses
> equipment in Jersey for processing the data otherwise than for the
> purposes of transit through Jersey; or
> (c) by a controller or processor not established in Jersey where the
> processing -
> (i) relates to data subjects who are in Jersey, and
> (ii) is for the purpose of offering goods or services to persons in
> Jersey or monitoring the behaviour of such persons.
> (3) A controller referred to in paragraph (2)(b) must nominate, in
> writing and for the purposes of this Law, a representative established in
> Jersey.

## Application

A controller with no Jersey establishment and no Jersey equipment, offering
goods/services to Jersey residents, falls within scope under 4(2)(c) alone.
The Article 4(3) representative duty is textually limited to paragraph
(2)(b) controllers (equipment-based presence) and does not extend to a
(2)(c)-only controller.
