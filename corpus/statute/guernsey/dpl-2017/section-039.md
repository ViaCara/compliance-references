# Guernsey DPL 2017 section 39 - Registration (curated quotes)

_Data Protection (Bailiwick of Guernsey) Law, 2017. Official text at the
source URI. Reviewed 2026-09-18 by kylewelsby._

## Section 39(1)-(3)

> 39. (1) A controller or processor established in the Bailiwick must not
> cause or permit personal data to be processed unless the controller or (as
> the case may be) processor is -
> (a) registered in accordance with Schedule 4, or
> (b) exempt from registration by regulations made by the Committee.
> (2) Schedule 4 has effect.
> (3) A person who fails to comply with or contravenes subsection (1) is
> guilty of an offence.

## Application

The registration duty is gated on establishment "in the Bailiwick" (s.39(1)).
A controller with no Guernsey establishment, in scope only through Condition
B (`section-002.md`), does not trigger this duty on that basis alone.
