# S.I. 2023/1028 reg. 4

_Independent supervisory authorities_

The independent supervisory authorities(1) for the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework are—

(a) the United States Federal Trade Commission; and

(b) the United States Department of Transportation.

Referred to in Article 45(2)(b) of the UK GDPR.
