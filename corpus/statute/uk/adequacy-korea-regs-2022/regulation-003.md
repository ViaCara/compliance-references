# S.I. 2022/1213 reg. 3

_Independent supervisory authorities_

The independent supervisory authorities(1) in the Republic of Korea are—

(a) the Personal Information Protection Commission established by Article 7 of the Personal Information Protection Act as it forms part of the law of the Republic of Korea; and

(b) the Financial Services Commission established by Article 3 of the Act on the Establishment of Financial Services Commission as it forms part of the law of the Republic of Korea(2).
