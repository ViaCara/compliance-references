# SVGA 2006, Sch. 3 para. 7

_Barred lists_

**Part 2 - Adults' barred list**

7(1) This paragraph applies to a person if any of the criteria prescribed for the purposes of this paragraph is satisfied in relation to the person.

(2) If DBS is satisfied that this paragraph applies to a person, it must include the person in the adults’ barred list.
