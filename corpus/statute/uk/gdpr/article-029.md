# UK GDPR Article 29

_Processing under the authority of the controller or processor_

**Chapter:** CHAPTER IV - Controller and processor

The processor and any person acting under the authority of the controller or of the processor, who has access to personal data, shall not process those data except on instructions from the controller, unless required to do so by domestic law.
